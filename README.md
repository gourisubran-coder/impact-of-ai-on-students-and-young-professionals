# Impact of AI on Students & Young Professionals | Power BI Dashboard

Understanding how Artificial Intelligence is transforming education, productivity, and career opportunities becomes easier when real survey responses are converted into meaningful insights through data analytics and interactive visualization.

I recently developed this **Impact of AI on Students & Young Professionals Dashboard using Power BI** to explore AI adoption, usage patterns, productivity, learning experiences, career confidence, and perceptions about AI replacing human jobs.

## Project Workflow

1. **Survey Data Collection:** Created a Google Form to collect primary survey responses from students and young professionals about their AI usage, learning experiences, productivity, and career perceptions.

2. **Data Export:** Exported the collected responses from Google Forms through Google Sheets and converted the dataset into CSV format.

3. **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Imported the CSV dataset into Power BI and used Power Query to clean and prepare the data by handling missing values, checking duplicate respondent IDs, trimming and cleaning text fields, correcting data types, and transforming multiple-response columns.

4. **Data Modeling:** Created additional analytical tables for AI Use Cases and AI Concerns, established relationships between tables, and organized the data model for accurate analysis.

5. **DAX & KPI Development:** Created DAX measures to calculate respondent counts, AI adoption metrics, active AI learners, productivity impact, career confidence, and other key performance indicators.

6. **Dashboard Development:** Designed interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboards using KPI cards, charts, treemaps, slicers, and other visualizations to present meaningful insights.

## Objective

The objective of this project is to transform primary survey data into meaningful insights about AI adoption and its influence on students and young professionals. It provides a consolidated view of AI usage, productivity, learning experiences, career perceptions, and future skill readiness to help identify emerging trends and understand how individuals are adapting to the growing influence of Artificial Intelligence.
